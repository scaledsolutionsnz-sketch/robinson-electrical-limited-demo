Privacy Policy

Placeholder content for the demo site. The final policy is supplied before launch.

1. What we collect

When you contact Robinson Electrical Limited we collect the details you give us, such as your name, phone number, email address and the property address for the job.

2. How we use it

Your details are used to quote, schedule and complete electrical work, to issue invoices and certification, and to contact you about the job. We do not sell or share your information for marketing.

3. Storage

Records are kept securely and only for as long as needed for the job, warranty periods and legal requirements such as electrical certification.

4. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. Ring 06 765 8167 and we will sort it.
